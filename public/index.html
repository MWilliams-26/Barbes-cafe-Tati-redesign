Two houses, one family

In Paris, you can’t have Barbès without Tati.

For seventy years, the Tati store anchored the boulevard in Barbès, the neighbourhood at the foot of Sacré-Cœur where France and North Africa share a table. In Hoboken, one family runs both: Café Tati on 4th & Bloomfield from first light, Barbès on 13th & Park into the night.

Each site tells its own story and sells its own room, then hands guests to the other at the right moment of the day.
