In Paris, you can’t have Barbès without Tati.

For seventy years, Tati’s pink-and-white gingham hung over the boulevard in Barbès, the neighbourhood at the foot of Sacré-Cœur where France and North Africa share a table. In Hoboken, one family runs both: Barbès on 13th & Park for the evening, and Café Tati on 4th & Bloomfield for the morning.

Each site tells its own story and sells its own room, then hands guests to the other at the right moment of the day.
