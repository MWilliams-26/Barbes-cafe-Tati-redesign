The name

Why Barbès

Barbès is the Paris quarter at the foot of Sacré-Cœur where France and North Africa share the same cafés, markets and tables. We named our bistro for it.

In Paris

A melting pot of Parisian and North African culture, cuisine, music and décor. Our menu takes its lead from Morocco, balanced with the classics of a Paris brasserie.

In Hoboken

Omar, from France, and Abdellah, from Morocco, opened the first Barbès in Murray Hill. When that building was sold, they brought it home to Hoboken. Since 2018 it has stood on the corner of 13th and Park.

“Merci de votre visite.”Abdellah & Omar
